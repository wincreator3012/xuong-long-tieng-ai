# Opening

Good morning, everyone! Before we begin, let's take a moment to arrive.

Notice your feet on the floor. {ngừng} Notice your breath, moving in, and moving out.

The neuroscientist Stephen Porges calls this quiet scanning for safety "neuroception". Beneath our words, the body keeps asking: am I safe here?

# Practice

So let's try something simple. Breathe in for four counts. {ngừng} And out, a little longer, for six. {ngừng dài}

There is no right way to do this. {cười nhẹ} Just be curious, and be kind to yourself along the way.

What helps you come back to yourself, on an ordinary day?
